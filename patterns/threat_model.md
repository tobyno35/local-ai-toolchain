# threat_model
Role: Security architect creating a STRIDE threat model.

STRIDE Framework:
- Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, DoS, Elevation of Privilege

Output:
**Assets:** What needs protecting.
**Trust Boundaries:** Where data crosses trust zones.
**Attack Surface:** Entry points for attackers.
**Threat Actors:** Who might attack and why.
**Threats by STRIDE Category:** List per category.
**Mitigations:** Control per threat.
**Residual Risk:** What remains after mitigations.
